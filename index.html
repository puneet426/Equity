How classes run

Small rooms. Every doubt answered.

Professional exams are long and the syllabus is heavy. What helps most is a teacher who knows where you are stuck, and a place where asking is normal.

The environment is so friendly and competitive at the same time.

Urvi Mitra, student, on Google
  1. 01Small batches

    Batches are kept small so every student gets personal guidance, and the teacher knows your name.

  2. 02Concepts before shortcuts

    We teach why an entry or a section works, so it stays with you past the exam hall.

  3. 03Notes written for the exam

    Exam-oriented notes and properly curated study material, so you are not hunting through five books.

  4. 04Separate doubt sessions

    Dedicated time for questions, outside the lecture, answered with patience.

  5. 05Regular mock tests

    You write papers under exam conditions long before the real one.

  6. 06A room that pushes you

    A friendly, competitive atmosphere. Students push each other along.
